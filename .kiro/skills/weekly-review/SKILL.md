# Weekly review

1. Review notes created or modified in the last seven days.
2. Read the calendar cache for the next fourteen days.
3. Summarize completed work, open tasks, Community Builder commitments, article opportunities, and learning goals.
4. Choose exactly three priorities and explain why they matter.
5. Write the result in `Briefings/`; link to sources with Obsidian wikilinks.
6. Do not modify source notes or calendar events.
