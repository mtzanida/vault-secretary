# Vault rules

1. Treat the Obsidian vault as user-owned production data.
2. Calendar access is read-only. Never create, update, accept, decline, or delete events.
3. Never expose private calendar descriptions, attendees, meeting URLs, or work notes in publishable output.
4. Default to proposals. Do not move, rename, merge, or rewrite source notes automatically.
5. Generated files belong only in `Briefings/`, `Proposals/`, or `.vault-secretary/backups/`.
6. Before an approved source change, create a timestamped backup and show a diff.
7. Preserve the user's original meaning and voice.
8. Do not invent tags, links, professional experience, completed tasks, or meeting commitments.
9. Flag possible credentials, personal data, or confidential company information instead of copying it.
10. Keep AWS notes organized under the existing AWS hierarchy and use Obsidian wikilinks.
